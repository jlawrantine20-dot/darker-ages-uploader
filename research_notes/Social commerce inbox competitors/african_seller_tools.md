# African (esp. Nigerian) social-commerce and small-seller tools that compete with Biztom

Research date: 26 Sep 2026. Method note: company sites (getbumpa.com, catlog.shop, take.app) were blocked by the network egress proxy, so everything below comes from search-result snippets of those pages plus press coverage. Treat prices as "reported by search index" and re-check them on the live pricing pages. Roughly 20 search calls were made; several named platforms could not be covered (see Gaps).

## Q0. Biztom itself: what is publicly available?

### Takeaway
Two differently phrased searches found no public trace of Biztom: no website, no press coverage and no app-store listing were indexed. It is either very early or stealth, or the name/domain differs from "Biztom".

- A search for "Biztom Nigeria WhatsApp Instagram inbox sellers" returned only generic Nigerian WhatsApp-selling guides (Techpoint, The Condia, OffaBuy, Stur) and nothing on Biztom — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/general/online-store-business-whatsapp-instagram-nigeria/), [OffaBuy blog](https://offabuy.com/blog)

### Inferences

## Q1. Platform profiles (features, pricing, market, traction, channels)

### Takeaway
The Nigerian field splits three ways. (a) Store/ops suites: Bumpa, Catlog, Take App, Paystack/Flutterwave storefronts. (b) Newer AI chat-sales assistants: Loop56, Tradelynk, Revora (ex-MyAlice), Musskart, Lura. (c) Creator/digital-product checkouts: Selar, Mainstack. Several once-relevant players are dead or have pivoted: Kippa, Sabi, and Chpter (absorbed into Cloud9).

### Cited Findings

**Bumpa (getbumpa.com): Nigeria, expanding to Ghana and Kenya. Active and the market leader.**
- Plans reported as Starter ₦5,000/mo, Pro ₦10,000/mo, Growth ₦25,000/mo. They are billed quarterly, bi-annually or annually (Growth only bi-annual/annual; Growth ≈ ₦250,000/yr), with a 14-day trial and payment in Naira or USD — [Bumpa pricing (via search)](https://www.getbumpa.com/pricing), [Bumpa support: subscription plans](https://support.getbumpa.com/support/solutions/articles/150000031628-bumpa-subscription-plans). A 2025 update added "Bumpa Scale" and "Bumpa Premium" tiers with multi-location, staff management and international pricing — [TechCabal, Jan 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/01/05/bumpa-milestone-2025/). Exact current tier names and prices conflict across sources and need re-checking.
- Bumpa's own positioning covers inventory, payments, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp orders, customer messaging, logistics and analytics in one platform, priced in Naira — [Bumpa vs Shopify blog](https://www.getbumpa.com/blog/bumpa-vs-shopify)
- Features: online and offline payments, inventory, bookkeeping, order fulfilment, requesting dispatch riders, customer engagement, all from a smartphone — [TechCabal seed coverage](https://techcabal.com/2022/10/19/nigerian-social-commerce-startup-bumpa-raises-4-million-seed-round/)
- Funding: $200k pre-seed (2021) and a $4M seed (Oct 2022) — [TechCabal 2021](https://techcabal.com/2021/09/02/bumpa-raises-200k-pre-seed/), [TechCabal 2022](https://techcabal.com/2022/10/19/nigerian-social-commerce-startup-bumpa-raises-4-million-seed-round/). In May 2026 it launched Bumpa Capital (SME credit) with Vendorcredit — [TechCabal May 2026](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/08/bumpa-and-vendorcredit-partner-to-launch-bumpa-capital/). It has expanded into Kenya — [Business Today KE](https://businesstoday.co.ke/nigerias-king-of-social-commerce-bumpa-invades-kenyas-software-market/). It partners with the Bank of Industry to give 200 youth MSMEs free digital tools — [TechCabal Nov 2025](https://techcabal.com/2025/11/03/bumpa-empowers-msmses/)
- Traction: 50k+ businesses, 200k+ orders and $20M GMV at the time of the seed round. Later sources cite about 100k users (the later figure is not strongly sourced) — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2022/10/19/nigerian-social-commerce-startup-bumpa-raises-4-million-seed-round/), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bumpa/__7yxl1invDfyNX0ovLpjXRZf6vEIs22cbnqNf_d0mfSU)
- The product is still shipping: a "July 2026 product updates" post exists (contents not retrieved) — [Bumpa blog](https://www.getbumpa.com/blog/bumpa-july-2026-product-updates). Website extensions — [Bumpa blog](https://www.getbumpa.com/blog/your-online-store-your-way-introducing-bumpa-website-extensions)
- Complaints (App Store and Play Store): settlements withheld or delayed and withdrawals declined over KYC/compliance checks, with poor support responsiveness; a product-photo deletion bug; install and update glitches. Bumpa does reply to reviews — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bumpa-manage-orders-easily/id1497638594?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.salescabal.app), [AllBioHub review 2026](https://allbiohub.com/bumpa-nigeria-app-review-kelvin-umechukwu-story/)

**Catlog (catlog.shop): Nigeria and Ghana. Active; Techstars NYC '24.**
- Store links that route buyer orders into WhatsApp chat; order and customer management; invoice links, PDFs and receipts; payments — [TechCabal 2022](https://techcabal.com/2022/04/07/catlog-wants-to-improve-social-commerce-in-nigeria/), [Catlog LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/company/myshopcatlog)
- Ghana launch: payments via Mobile Money and debit card, plus invoice links — [Catlog LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/company/myshopcatlog)
- Pricing (reported; the date is unclear and Catlog has changed pricing several times). Basic: ₦2,350/mo, ₦6,695/quarter, ₦12,970/6 months. Business+: ₦6,750/mo, ₦19,235/quarter, ₦37,560/6 months. Discounts are 5% quarterly and 8% bi-annual — [Catlog blog: new pricing](https://blog.catlog.shop/catlogs-new-pricing/), [Important updates to pricing](https://blog.catlog.shop/important-updates-to-catlogs-pricing/)
- Founded 2022 by Silas Adedoyin, Lagos; joined Techstars (2024) with a stated aim to scale across Africa and LatAm — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/catlog/___3OKDzcpFUklnxeX7stdqsSKh6guHT3YU3kTw43vHzE), [Adedoyin LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/silasadedoyin_catlog-is-now-part-of-the-techstars-family-activity-7254493985669365761-RMtp)

**Take App (take.app): global (180+ countries, 15 languages), strong in Africa and SE Asia. Active.**
- A WhatsApp-linked store builder covering products, payments, delivery and inventory. It is free to start with no commission on orders. The first paid plan costs $1 for the first month, then $50/mo, per its pricing page as indexed — [Take App pricing](https://www.take.app/pricing), [Take App home](https://www.take.app/)
- Nigerian payment options: bank transfer, cash on delivery, mobile money, store credit. It has country and payment pages for South Africa (ZAR), M-Pesa and pawaPay — [Take App FAQ](https://www.take.app/help/faq), [M-Pesa page](https://www.take.app/payments/m-pesa), [pawaPay page](https://www.take.app/payments/pawapay)
- At $50/mo (about ₦75k+ at 2026 rates) it is expensive for Nigerian micro-sellers compared with Bumpa or Catlog. This is an inference and was not verified against a Naira price list.

**Paystack Storefront / Paystack Commerce: NG, GH, ZA. Still live.**
- Storefront is free and enabled for all Paystack merchants in South Africa, Ghana and Nigeria. It has a public Storefront API — [Paystack support](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2132738), [Storefront API docs](https://paystack.com/docs/api/storefront/), [paystack.com/storefront](https://paystack.com/storefront)
- It is a checkout and catalog tool only, with no inbox or CRM. That is an inference from the product pages.

**Flutterwave Store: status uncertain.**
- Launched during COVID and said to have enrolled 20k+ businesses for free. No shutdown announcement was found. Flutterwave did shut Barter (Mar 2024) and pause Disha (Mar 2024) — [Wikipedia: Flutterwave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flutterwave)
- A 2026 WeeTracker piece shows both fintechs moving into consumer banking rather than merchant storefronts — [WeeTracker Apr 2026](https://weetracker.com/2026/04/07/flutterwave-paystack-consumer-banking-microfinance-license/)

**Loop56 (loop56.com): Nigeria. Active. The closest AI-inbox competitor.**
- An AI Sales Assistant across Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger, with a unified inbox. It answers inquiries, recommends catalog products with prices and images, creates orders, calculates delivery fees, generates invoices and sends payment links in-chat. It speaks English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa. The site claims 2,300+ Nigerian businesses — [Loop56 AI Sales Assistant](https://loop56.com/features/ai-sales-assistant)
- The home page positions it as "POS, Inventory, Banking & AI Sales Assistant" — [loop56.com](https://loop56.com/)

**Tradelynk (tradelynk.app): Nigeria-oriented. Active.**
- An Instagram-seller storefront plus a 24/7 WhatsApp AI sales assistant that learns products and prices and handles conversations. Pricing is not disclosed ("less than what you lose from one missed sale") — [Tradelynk](https://www.tradelynk.app/)

- In the Africa-focused sources found here, no competitor explicitly advertises per-customer product-interest tagging with automatic back-in-stock or price-drop alerts over WhatsApp, Instagram or SMS. That looks like Biztom's clearest differentiator, but absence of evidence is not proof. ## Q4. Pricing and SME complaints

### Takeaway
Nigerian SME tools cluster at roughly ₦2k–₦25k per month. Take App ($50) and API-based AI bots (≈₦90–100k/mo growth tiers) sit well above that. Complaints centre on payouts/KYC, support, bugs and cost.

### Cited Findings
- Catlog from ₦2,350/mo; Bumpa ₦5k–25k/mo; Selar 4% + ₦50 per sale — sources above
- Flat-Naira WhatsApp automation growth tiers of about ₦90k–100k/mo. Meta's Nigeria WhatsApp API rates are marketing $0.0516, utility $0.0067 and authentication $0.0145 per message/conversation — [NaijaTechGuide](https://www.naijatechguide.com/whatsapp-business-api-price-guide.html), [Ominiflow](https://ominiflow.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/nigeria)

### Inferences
- Restock alerts sent as WhatsApp *marketing* templates cost about $0.05 each in Nigeria. As *utility* templates (for example, a transactional alert the customer opted into) they cost about $0.007, if Meta classifies them that way. This drives the unit economics of Biztom-style alerts. SMS is a cheaper fallback.

## Q5. AI features

### Takeaway
AI is concentrated in newer entrants (Loop56, Tradelynk, Revora, Musskart, Lura, and formerly Chpter). No Bumpa or Catlog AI chat feature was confirmed in the sources found. A regulatory caveat applies: WhatsApp's terms bar general-purpose chatbots on the Business API.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp changed its Business API terms to bar general-purpose AI chatbots (announced Oct 2025). Business-specific customer-service bots remain allowed — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/)
- Loop56's local-language AI sales agent — [Loop56](https://loop56.com/features/ai-sales-assistant)
