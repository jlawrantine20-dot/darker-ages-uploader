# Customer acquisition for small sellers: cold outreach rules, listening sources, CTWA ads (as of Sept 2026)

Method note: whatsappbusiness.com, grey.co and most developers.facebook.com pages were blocked by the network proxy. Findings below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages. Treat figures as "reported" unless marked otherwise. Naira conversions use the report's implied rate (~₦1,355/$, from $0.062 ≈ ₦84).

## 1. Cold WhatsApp / Instagram outreach

### Takeaway
There is no policy-compliant, lawful way to cold-message scraped numbers on WhatsApp in Nigeria. WhatsApp requires a business-specific opt-in, and NDPA/GAID require consent for direct marketing, including to business contacts. A Federal High Court has ordered GTCO to stop unsolicited marketing to non-customers.

### Cited findings
- WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy: opt-in must clearly state the person is opting in to receive messages and must name the business; users can block/report messages they find "irrelevant, unexpected or unwanted"; with significant negative feedback WhatsApp "may limit the number of messages" or remove access to WhatsApp Business Services — [WhatsApp policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) (via search extract, page blocked); [Meta: Get opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in); [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/blog/how-to-collect-whatsapp-business-opt-ins)
- Quality rating is based on blocks, reports and block reasons over the past 7 days, weighted to recent; low quality for a sustained period leads to rate limiting and template pausing — [cm.com](https://www.cm.com/blog/whatsapp-opt-in/); [Whatsable](https://whatsable.app/blog/whatsapp-spam-policy-explained-for-businesses-in-2026)
- Messaging limits: 250 / 2,000 (or 1,000 start for verified) / 10,000 / 100,000 / unlimited unique users per rolling 24h; since Oct 2025 shared across all numbers in a Business Portfolio; drops one tier after 7 days at Low quality — [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/whats-app-messaging-limits); [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-message-limits-guide/); [Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits)
- Unofficial bulk senders, Chrome extensions and modded clients are detected and banned, reportedly with no appeal; vendors claim 95%+ of bans are for unauthorised bulk/automated messaging (vendor claim, unverified) — [Zaple](https://zaple.ai/blog/whatsapp-bulk-messages-avoid-ban/); [WAWCD](https://wawcd.com/blog/why-whatsapp-accounts-get-banned)
- Instagram: third-party estimates put message requests to non-followers at ~10–20/day for new accounts before flags; fast identical sends trigger temporary DM blocks (12–24h+) — [Flowgent](https://flowgent.ai/blog/instagram-dm-limits-how-many-messages-you-can-send-daily); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-limits/). Not an official Meta figure.
- GAID 2025 (in force 19 Sept 2025): consent required for direct marketing (Art. 18); consent explicit, informed, freely given (Art. 17); business contact information of individuals is personal data, NDPA applies to B2B — [Adeola Oyinlade & Co](https://www.adeolaoyinlade.com/en/key-regulatory-updates-under-the-2025-general-application-and-implementation-directive-gaid-issued-by-the-nigeria-data-protection-commission-ndpc/); [OTL Law](https://otllaw.com/article-6-ndpa-gaid/); [NDPC GAID PDF](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf)
- Federal High Court ordered GTCO to cease unsolicited marketing to non-customers — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/privacy-protection/1823398/court-orders-gtco-to-cease-unsolicited-marketing-to-non-customers-over-data-privacy-breach); [OAL](https://oal.law/court-orders-gtco-to-cease-unsolicited-marketing-to-non-customers-over-data-privacy-breach/)
- NDPC fined Fidelity Bank ₦555.8m for processing without informed consent; MultiChoice ₦766.2m; 1,300+ organisations investigated Aug 2025; NDPA penalties up to ₦10m or 2% of annual gross revenue — [Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/02/poor-data-privacy-how-online-scams-unsolicited-ads-score-nigerias-digital-economy-low/amp/); [Web Security Lab](https://websecuritylab.org/ndpc-orders-1300-firms-to-prove-compliance-with-nigerias-data-protection-act/)
- NCC: networks face ₦5m fines for unwanted messages (SMS DND context, not researched in depth) — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/news/networks-unwanted-message-5-million-fine/)

## 2. Click-to-WhatsApp / Click-to-Instagram ads

- Free entry point: when a customer messages via a CTWA ad or Facebook Page CTA button and the business replies within 24h, all messages (including templates) are free for 72 hours — [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-pricing-change-2026); [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-guide/); [Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- Oct 2026 changes (service messages charged after 1,000 free/number/month; in-window utility charged) do not change the 72h free entry point per aggregators — [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-pricing-update-effective-october-1-2026); [Ominiflow](https://ominiflow.com/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-update-october-2026). Meta's page not accessed.
- Marketing API: ad sets with destination_type=WHATSAPP; also Click to Instagram, Click to Messenger and multidestination; inbound webhook carries a referral object with source ad ID and ctwa_clid — [Meta Marketing API: Click to WhatsApp](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp); [MetricFixer](https://metricfixer.com/publications/analytics-conversion-tracking/track-click-to-whatsapp-ads-campaign-ad-creative-without-website)
- Nigeria Meta CPM ~$1.50, lowest among countries listed (global avg $6.59) — [AdAmigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026)
- Nigerian blogs: ₦200–1,000+ per messaging result; link clicks ₦30–70 — [360 Hub](https://360hub.club/facebook-advertising-cost-in-nigeria/); [Grey](https://grey.co/blog/facebook-ads-cost-nigeria) (blocked; attribution between these from search snippet, unverified)

## 3. Social listening sources

- X API: pay-per-use since Feb 2026; ~$0.005 per post read, $0.015 per post created; capped at 3M reads/month before Enterprise; legacy Basic/Pro migrated in 2026 — [X docs](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing); [Postproxy](https://postproxy.dev/blog/x-api-pricing-2026/); [Outstand](https://www.outstand.so/blog/x-api-pricing)
- X automation rules: automated replies based on keyword searches alone are not permitted; automated replies only where the account was @mentioned/quoted in that post (since 23 Feb 2026, per secondary source) — [X Help](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation); [X dev community](https://devcommunity.x.com/t/policy-clarification-automated-replies-and-mentions/94444)
- Facebook Groups API removed 22 April 2024; no third-party read of group feeds — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/05/meta-cuts-off-third-party-access-to-facebook-groups-leaving-developers-and-customers-in-disarray/); [Sprinklr](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/getting-started-facebook/meta-deprecates-facebook-groups-api/66229eb25f9dd9599d632712)
- Meta Content Library: academic/non-profit public-interest researchers only — [Meta Transparency](https://transparency.meta.com/researchtools/meta-content-library/)
- Instagram hashtag search (Graph API): 30 unique hashtags per rolling 7 days per IG user; no PII in responses; cannot comment on media found via API; 200 calls/hour — [Meta reference](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/instagram-api/reference/ig-user/recently_searched_hashtags); [Emplifi](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/instagram-hashtag-limitation-faq); [Phyllo](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/how-to-use-instagram-api-to-pull-photos-based-on-hashtag)
- TikTok Research API: academic/non-profit in limited regions; commercial users ineligible — [TikTok developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/products/research-api/); [TikTok Research ToS](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/terms-of-service-research-api/en)
- Nairaland: no official API found; third-party scrapers on Apify; terms of service not retrieved — [Apify](https://apify.com/logiover/nairaland-scraper/api/mcp)
- Brand24: Individual $199/mo annual ($249 monthly), up from $79; Team $299, Pro $399, Business $599 — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/brand24/); [MentionKit](https://mentionkit.com/articles/brand24-pricing)
- Mention: Solo/Pro/Pro Plus discontinued July 2025; Company plan from $599/mo annual — [Mention help](https://en.support.mention.com/en/articles/7988097-mention-plans-explained); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/mention-pricing)
- No Nigerian self-serve listening product with public pricing found; Nigerian "listening" results were agencies (Sortlist) — [Sortlist](https://www.sortlist.com/s/social-media-listening/nigeria-ng)

## 4. Comment-to-DM, links, QR, referrals

- Instagram Private Replies: one private reply per comment, comment up to 7 days old, bypasses 24h window for that first message; ManyChat uses this official API — [Postproxy](https://postproxy.dev/how-to/instagram-comment-to-dm-private-reply/); [ManyChat help](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/14281316989724-Instagram-Post-and-Reel-Comments-trigger)
- WhatsApp Business QR codes and wa.me links with prefilled text — [WhatsApp Help](https://faq.whatsapp.com/888878128766436)
- respond.io Growth Widgets: chat links, QR codes, website widgets, forms, click-to-chat ad workflows, with source tracking — [respond.io](https://respond.io/help/workspace-settings/growth-widgets); [respond.io entry points](https://respond.io/help/whatsapp/grow-your-whatsapp-audience-by-maximizing-entry-points)
- Wati: CTWA ads integration; free link and QR generators — [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/best-whatsapp-marketing-software/)
- Bumpa: cannot run ads from the app; supports Facebook pixel and Google Analytics on Bumpa websites — [Bumpa FAQ](https://www.getbumpa.com/faq); [Bumpa connected apps](https://www.getbumpa.com/feature/connected-apps). Bumpa referral programme is merchant-to-merchant (up to ₦120k/month) — [Bumpa support](https://support.getbumpa.com/support/solutions/articles/150000068548-bumpa-referral-program)
- Loop56, Take App: no acquisition features (ads, listening, referral) found.

## 5. B2B prospecting

- Google Maps Platform: Places content must not be pre-fetched, cached or stored except place_id (and limited 30-day exceptions); since March 2025 free monthly caps per SKU (10k Essentials, 5k Pro, 1k Enterprise); $2–40 per 1,000 requests — [Google Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies); [Maps service terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms); [Woosmap](https://www.woosmap.com/blog/google-maps-api-pricing-breakdown)
- BusinessList.com.ng terms forbid commercial exploitation of site data and use for junk email/spamming — [BusinessList terms](https://www.businesslist.com.ng/terms-of-use). VConnect terms not checked — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VConnect)
- LinkedIn User Agreement bans third-party automation/scraping; Q1 2026 ~40% of accounts on flagged tools restricted; HeyReach dropped LinkedIn automation in March 2026 (secondary, vendor-authored) — [Leadium](https://www.leadium.com/blog/linkedin-outreach-automation); [Northlight](https://northlight.ai/blog/linkedin-automation-without-getting-banned)
- Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (>5,000/day): SPF, DKIM, DMARC, one-click unsubscribe for marketing, spam rate <0.3%; Gmail stepped up rejections from Nov 2025 — [Gmail FAQ](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414?hl=en); [Security Boulevard](https://securityboulevard.com/2025/11/google-and-yahoo-updated-email-authentication-requirements-for-2025/)
