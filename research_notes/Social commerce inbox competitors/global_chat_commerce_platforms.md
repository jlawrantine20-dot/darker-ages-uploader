# Global WhatsApp / Omnichannel Shared-Inbox and Chat-Commerce Platforms (state as of Sept 2026)

Method note: official vendor sites (wati.io, respond.io, manychat.com, zoko.io, developers.facebook.com, help.superchat.com, techcabal.com, businessday.ng, saharareporters.com) were blocked by the research environment's egress proxy, so figures below come from search-result extracts of vendor pages, review sites (G2/Capterra/Trustpilot) and third-party pricing breakdowns. Many third-party breakdowns are written by competing vendors (Chatarmin, Zoko, respond.io, SetSmart, BossBot etc.), so treat them with skepticism and re-verify prices on the vendor pages before relying on them. Prices are annual-billing USD unless stated.

## Q1. Platform profiles: features, pricing, channels, Nigeria/Africa, complaints

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) India-born, low-priced WhatsApp-API-first tools with strong Shopify/commerce hooks (Wati, Interakt, AiSensy, Gallabox, Zoko, DoubleTick); (b) omnichannel inbox/CRM platforms at $79–$500/month (respond.io, SleekFlow, Trengo, Kommo, Superchat); (c) DM/bot automation tools billed per contact (ManyChat, Chatfuel); (d) enterprise European commerce tools (Charles); and (e) raw APIs (Twilio). Almost all charge a subscription plus either a markup or pass-through on Meta's per-message fees, and the most common complaint across all of them is bills far above the headline price.

## Q2. Table stakes vs differentiators; who offers back-in-stock / price-drop / waitlist natively

### Takeaway
Shared inbox, tags/custom attributes, broadcasts, template management, a no-code flow builder, Shopify/WooCommerce sync, abandoned-cart flows and some form of AI replies are now table stakes. Back-in-stock and price-drop alerts are mostly NOT native in inbox platforms; they are served by Charles (enterprise) and by standalone Shopify "notify me" apps. That leaves an open niche for an SMB, non-Shopify, interest-tag-driven restock/price alert product, which is what Biztom does.

### Cited Findings
- AiSensy Pro tier gates tags (up to 100) and custom attributes (20), showing tags/attributes are core packaging levers — [Chatmitra](https://chatmitra.com/chatmitra-vs-aisensy/)
- respond.io gates broadcasts, workflows and AI Agents to Growth ($159) — [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/respond-io-pricing)
- Zoko: abandoned cart, order updates, COD confirmation pre-built — [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/zoko-alternative)
- Charles: abandoned cart, restock reminders and back-in-stock notifications natively — [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/charles-alternative)
- Standalone Shopify apps offer back-in-stock + price-drop via WhatsApp: "Stok: Back in Stock Alert" (email, SMS, WhatsApp; notify-me widgets; price-drop), "Back In Stock Notify Pro" (WhatsApp/SMS/push; waitlists and price-drop requests), "Back In Stock WhatsApp Alerts" (opt-in widget on out-of-stock pages) — [Shopify: Stok](https://apps.shopify.com/notifyme), [Shopify: Notify Pro](https://apps.shopify.com/back-in-stock-notify-pro), [Shopify: WhatsApp Alerts](https://apps.shopify.com/discocrate-whatsapp-business)
- AI differentiators emerging: SleekFlow bundles AI agent into plans; ManyChat charges $29 extra; Trengo charges per AI conversation; Kommo Pro adds AI voice-note handling and appointment booking; DoubleTick image-recognition-to-cart — [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sleekflow-pricing), [SetSmart](https://setsmart.io/blog/manychat-pricing), [eesel Trengo](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/trengo-pricing), [Kommo](https://www.kommo.com/blog/kommo-pricing/), [Capterra DoubleTick](https://www.capterra.com/p/10003201/Double-Tick/)
- Proactive checkout chatbots cited as recovering 20–25% of otherwise-lost carts (vendor-style claim) — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/21/whatsapp-chatbots/)
- Meta's own free Business Agent (product recommendation, lead qualification) commoditizes basic AI auto-replies — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/metas-ai-agent-for-whatsapp-business-is-now-available-globally/)

### Inferences
- Differentiators for a Biztom successor: interest-tagging from conversations (auto-extracted by LLM), native restock/price-drop/waitlist triggers that work without Shopify (Instagram/WhatsApp-only sellers), Paystack/Flutterwave links, naira pricing, and a mobile-first seller app.
- Basic AI FAQ replies are losing value as a paid feature given Meta Business Agent is free (for now).

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Wati, Interakt, Gallabox, SleekFlow or respond.io have a native back-in-stock/price-drop trigger (they may via Shopify webhook + workflow); no source found.

## Q3. Handling of Meta's 24-hour window and template message costs

### Takeaway
Since July 1, 2025 Meta charges per delivered template message by category and recipient country; from October 1, 2026 Meta also charges for in-window service replies and in-window utility templates (Nigeria ≈ $0.0101 / ₦14 each; marketing ≈ $0.062 / ₦84), with reports of a 1,000 free service messages/month per number. Vendors either pass through at cost (Trengo, respond.io-style), add a markup (Wati ~20%, Interakt, AiSensy, Charles), add a per-message platform fee (Zoko $0.015/conversation, Twilio $0.005/message), or absorb it via credit bundles (Superchat).

### Cited Findings
- July 1, 2025: per-message pricing by template category and recipient country code; utility templates inside an open customer-service window free — [Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/updates-to-pricing/)
- Volume tiers apply to Utility and Authentication only; marketing excluded; US marketing delivery paused since April 2025 — [Landbot](https://landbot.io/blog/twilio-whatsapp-pricing)
- Oct 1, 2026: service messages and in-window utility become chargeable; rates published by Sept 1, 2026 — [Landbot](https://landbot.io/blog/twilio-whatsapp-pricing); Germany service message €0.0456 — [Superchat](https://help.superchat.com/en/articles/769350-new-whatsapp-pricing-from-meta-starting-october-1-2026)
- Nigeria: ~$0.0101 (~₦14 at ₦1,340/$) per service or qualifying utility message; marketing ~$0.062 (~₦84); service messages stop delivering from Oct 1, 2026 for businesses without a payment method on file by Sept 30 — [Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/breakingnews/meta-to-charge-%E2%82%A614-per-whatsapp-message-from-october/), [TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/meta-to-charge-businesses-for-whatsapp-messages-from-october-1/), [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/technology/1728475-which-whatsapp-users-pay-n14-message-what-metas-billing-means-nigerians/)
- Free monthly tier of 1,000 service messages per business phone number reported — [WithinNigeria](https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/08/29/whatsapp-business-charges-what-nigerian-companies-need-to-know-before-october-1/) (single secondary source; verify on Meta docs)
- Ordinary users/free Business app users not affected; the charge is on the Business Platform (API) — [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/technology/1728475-which-whatsapp-users-pay-n14-message-what-metas-billing-means-nigerians/)
- Vendor handling: Wati ~20% markup; Trengo 1:1 pass-through; Zoko $0.015/conversation fee; Twilio $0.005/message; Superchat doubled credit bonus to absorb — sources in Q1.

### Inferences
- For Biztom-style restock/price-change alerts (business-initiated, outside 24h window), each alert is a marketing template at ~₦84 in Nigeria — this is the key unit-economics constraint. Designs that prompt customers to message first (click-to-WhatsApp ads, "reply YES to get notified" opt-ins, free entry points) and batch alerts, or use Instagram DMs/the free Business app where possible, materially reduce cost.
- Whether a restock alert qualifies as "utility" (₦14) vs "marketing" (₦84) depends on Meta's template categorization; back-in-stock requested by the user may arguably be utility but Meta commonly reclassifies promotional content — needs verification.

### Gaps
- Free entry point (72h window from CTWA ads) rules post-Oct 2026 not verified (Meta docs blocked).
- Instagram DM messaging costs/rules (24h window + human agent tag) not researched here.

## Q4. What small sellers find too expensive or complex

### Takeaway
Consistent pain points: multi-meter pricing (subscription + seats + contacts/MACs + message markup + AI credits + add-on flows) making the real bill several times the headline; cancellation/refund friction; slow email-only support; steep workflow-builder learning curves; and feature gating (broadcasts/automation/AI only on mid tiers). Instagram automation also carries account-ban risk.

### Cited Findings
- Wati: real bill ~5x headline; "every tiny bit is priced"; cancellation and refund disputes — [Flowcart](https://www.flowcart.ai/blog/wati-reviews), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wati.io)
- respond.io: MAC-based costs climb; complex setup; Starter lacks broadcasts/workflows/AI — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/respond-io/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons), [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/respond-io-pricing)
- SleekFlow: five meters, only two with published unit prices; 3-seat minimum — [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sleekflow-pricing), [BossBot](https://bossbot.uk/blog/sleekflow-pricing-review-2026)
- ManyChat: active-contact billing punishes engagement; AI extra; email-only support; IG bans — [Hack'celeration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/review/manychat)
- AiSensy: users surprised Meta charges are separate — [Zoko](https://www.zoko.io/post/whatsapp-business-api-aisensy)
- Trengo: fixed 10/20-user packages, nothing in between; AI per-conversation surcharge — [Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/trengo-pricing)
- Nigeria cost pressure: BusinessDay framed the ₦14 fee as costing MSMEs ₦14m per million messages — [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/whatsapps-n14-fee-could-cost-nigerian-msmes-n14m-per-million-messages/)
- NDPA: messaging non-opted-in (scraped/bought) numbers creates legal exposure — [Aikido](https://aikido.ng/thinking/whatsapp-marketing-nigeria/)

### Inferences
- A winning SMB product for Nigeria likely needs: flat naira pricing, message costs shown before send, prepaid wallet top-ups, a free/low tier using the Business app + Coexistence, and simple preset automations (restock, price drop, abandoned chat follow-up) instead of a blank flow builder.

### Gaps
- No Nigeria-specific user reviews of these global tools found; no data on Biztom's own pricing in this pass.
