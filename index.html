darker-ages-uploader

A private publishing tool for the Chronicles of the Darker Ages YouTube channel.

This is a small personal application. It publishes videos to a single YouTube channel owned by its operator, and reads that same channel's performance data.

What it does

  • Uploads video files to the operator's own YouTube channel
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish schedule for those uploads
  • Reads view, watch time and traffic data for that channel

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool runs locally, has no public interface, no sign-up, and no other users. It does not access any account other than the one that authorises it.
